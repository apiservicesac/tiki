What Tiki does

  • Captures a task or a reminder from one sentence, the way you would say it.
  • Organizes your work by project, with tags, follow-ups and a trash you can restore from.
  • Reminds you at the right moment: reminders, follow-ups, a morning brief, an end-of-day close and an alert before each meeting, with a Join button for video calls.
  • Knows when to keep quiet: alerts arrive without sound at night and during calls.

Your calendar

If you connect a Google or Microsoft 365 account, Tiki only reads your calendar events (title, time, place and video-call link) to show them in its Agenda and remind you before each meeting. Tiki never creates, changes or deletes events, and your calendar data stays on your computer. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Your data stays with you

Tiki runs on your computer and keeps everything in a local database. There is no Tiki server that receives your data, and nothing is sold or used for advertising.
