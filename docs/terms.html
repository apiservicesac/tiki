These terms apply to Tiki, a desktop assistant for Windows developed by API SERVICE SAC (GitHub: Josuesp1620). By installing or using Tiki you accept them.

1. What Tiki is

Tiki keeps your tasks, reminders and meetings in one place on your computer and reminds you of them. It can read the calendars you connect (Google or Microsoft 365) to show your meetings and alert you before they start. It never creates, changes or deletes calendar events.

2. Your account and your data

You connect your calendar accounts yourself and can disconnect them at any time from Tiki's settings or from your Google or Microsoft account. Your data stays on your computer, as described in the privacy policy, which is part of these terms.

3. Optional AI features

If you add your own key for an AI provider, Tiki sends that provider only what each feature needs, as the privacy policy explains. Your use of that provider is governed by its own terms.
