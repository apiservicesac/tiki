Tiki Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026 · Leer en español

Tiki is a desktop assistant for Windows that keeps your tasks, reminders and meetings in one place and reminds you of them at the right time. Tiki is developed by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we"). This policy explains what data Tiki accesses, how it is used, where it is stored and how you can delete it.

In short: Tiki runs on your computer. Your data stays on your computer. We do not run servers that receive your data, we do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising.

1. Data Tiki accesses

Data you create in Tiki. Tasks, reminders, projects, notes and settings you enter. They are stored only in a local database on your computer.

Google user data (only if you connect a Google account). When you choose "Connect Google" and give your consent, Tiki requests these permissions:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly: read-only access to the events of the calendars you show in Google Calendar.
  • openid and email: your email address, to show which account is connected.

From your calendar events Tiki reads only: title, start and end time, whether it lasts all day, location and video call link. Tiki reads events from today up to 8 days ahead.

Tiki never creates, changes or deletes calendar events, and it does not access Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google service.

2. How Tiki uses Google user data

Tiki uses calendar data only to provide features you see in the app:

  • show your meetings in the Agenda, next to your reminders;
  • alert you shortly before a meeting starts, with a button to join its video call;
  • tell you how much free time you have before your next meeting, and list the day's meetings in the morning summary.

Your email address is used only to label the connected account in Settings.

3. Where data is stored

  • Calendar events are copied to the local Tiki database on your computer, and refreshed every 5 minutes while Tiki is running.
  • The sign-in token that lets Tiki read your calendar is stored in Windows Credential Manager on your computer. It is never written to the Tiki database, shown in the interface or sent to us.
  • Tiki talks directly to Google's servers over HTTPS. Your Google data does not pass through any server of ours.

4. Sharing and transfer

We do not sell, rent, share or transfer Google user data to anyone. Specifically:

  • Google user data is not sent to us or any third party;
  • Google user data is not used for advertising, profiling or credit decisions;
  • Google user data is not sent to artificial intelligence providers and is not used to develop, improve or train AI or machine learning models;
  • no human reads your Google user data.

5. Optional AI features

Tiki can use an AI model to understand what you type (for example, turning "call Ana tomorrow at 10" into a reminder). This works only if you add your own API key from Anthropic or OpenAI in Settings. When you use it, the text you type and a summary of your open tasks and project notes are sent from your computer to the provider you chose, under that provider's terms. Calendar data from Google is not included in these requests. Your API key is stored in Windows Credential Manager.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

Tiki's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Meetings are kept only for the period Tiki displays; past meetings outside that period are removed on the next refresh.
  • In Tiki, Settings › Accounts › Disconnect deletes that account's meetings from the local database and its sign-in token from Windows Credential Manager.
  • Uninstalling Tiki and deleting its data folder removes everything Tiki stored.
  • You can also revoke Tiki's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

8. Updates

Tiki checks GitHub for new versions. That request contains no personal data, only what any web request contains (such as your IP address), and is handled by GitHub.

9. Security

Connections to Google use HTTPS and the OAuth 2.0 sign-in with PKCE. Tokens and keys are kept in Windows Credential Manager. Nothing leaves your computer without an action from you.

10. Children

Tiki is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.

Questions or requests about your data: [CONTACT EMAIL].

Política de privacidad de Tiki

Última actualización: 22 de octubre de 2026

Tiki es un asistente de escritorio para Windows que reúne tus tareas, recordatorios y reuniones, y te avisa en el momento oportuno. Lo desarrolla [DEVELOPER NAME].

En resumen: Tiki funciona en tu computadora y tus datos se quedan en ella. No tenemos servidores que reciban tus datos, no los vendemos y no los usamos para publicidad.

1. Datos a los que accede

Las tareas, recordatorios, proyectos, notas y ajustes que escribes, guardados solo en una base de datos local. Si conectas Google, Tiki pide calendar.readonly (solo lectura del calendario), openid y email. De cada evento lee título, inicio y fin, si es de todo el día, lugar y enlace de videollamada, desde hoy hasta 8 días después. Tiki nunca crea, cambia ni borra eventos, ni accede a Gmail, Drive, contactos u otros servicios.

2. Uso

Solo para mostrar tus reuniones en la Agenda, avisarte antes de cada una con un botón para unirte, decirte cuánto tiempo libre tienes antes de la próxima y listarlas en el resumen de la mañana. Tu correo solo identifica la cuenta conectada en Ajustes.

3. Almacenamiento

Los eventos se copian a la base local y se actualizan cada 5 minutos. El token de acceso se guarda en el Administrador de credenciales de Windows. Tiki se comunica directamente con Google por HTTPS; nada pasa por servidores nuestros.

4. Compartir

No vendemos, compartimos ni transferimos los datos de Google a nadie. No se usan para publicidad, no se envían a proveedores de inteligencia artificial, no se usan para entrenar modelos y ninguna persona los lee.

5. IA opcional

Si agregas tu propia clave de Anthropic u OpenAI, el texto que escribes y un resumen de tus tareas y notas de proyecto se envían a ese proveedor. Los datos del calendario de Google no se incluyen.

6. Política de Google

El uso y la transferencia de información recibida de las API de Google cumplen la Política de datos de usuario de los servicios de API de Google, incluidos los requisitos de Uso limitado.

7. Conservación y borrado

Ajustes › Cuentas › Desconectar borra las reuniones de esa cuenta y su token. Desinstalar Tiki y borrar su carpeta de datos elimina todo. También puedes revocar el acceso en myaccount.google.com/permissions.
